# F02.4 — `unwrapNode` only re-depths direct children; grandchildren mis-indented

**Prior verdict:** CONFIRMED (re-audited 2026-04-24; repro `observed`/`expected` text was rewritten to add a REFERENCE subtree)
**New verdict:** **CONFIRMED**
**Batch:** 02-transform | **Port:** 7605 | **Log:** `2026-04-24T17-44-27-00-00_F02.4-unwrap-main-grandchild-depth_LBuSmHKPZZNo5RV7XA8YUe.eval`

## What the finding claims

`transform.ts:203-208` — `unwrapNode` (fired by `unwrap_main` for `span.type === "main"`) sets `child.depth = node.depth` for direct children only; it does **not** recurse, so grandchildren keep their original (now too-deep) depth. Contrast `discardNode` at line 223 which uses recursive `reduceDepth`.

## What the repro's bug_description says to look for

> **Where:** Outline (left panel) or transcript: compare the left edge of `REFERENCE_correct_depth_2` against `BUGGY_GRANDCHILD_should_be_depth_2`. They are logically at the same level (both grandchildren of the solver root) so their left edges should align.
> **Observed:** `BUGGY_GRANDCHILD_should_be_depth_2` is indented **one extra step** (~16 px transcript / ~9.6 px outline) to the right of `REFERENCE_correct_depth_2`. `BUGGY_CHILD_depth_1` aligns with `REFERENCE_correct_depth_1` (unwrap fixed *it*), but its children jump straight to depth 3.
> **Expected:** `BUGGY_GRANDCHILD_should_be_depth_2` left-aligned with `REFERENCE_correct_depth_2` (both at depth 2).

## Analysis

**Yes — `BUGGY_GRANDCHILD_should_be_depth_2` is indented more than `REFERENCE_correct_depth_2`**, by exactly one indent unit in both surfaces (outline: `data-depth` 3 vs 2, +9.6 px; transcript: +16.0 px). The control comparison (`BUGGY_CHILD_depth_1` vs `REFERENCE_correct_depth_1`) shows perfect alignment, confirming `unwrapNode` correctly re-depthed the *direct* child but not the grandchild.

Source re-read confirms: `transform.ts:203-208` does `child.depth = node.depth` with no recursion; `discardNode` four lines below uses recursive `reduceDepth`, so the omission is not by design.

The **rewritten repro is now correct**. The old repro told reviewers to compare GRANDCHILD against its sibling INFO — both are equally mis-depthed, so they're flush regardless of the bug. The new REFERENCE subtree gives a known-good depth-2 anchor that *does* distinguish bug from no-bug, and the new `observed`/`expected` text matches what actually renders to within 0.1 px.

**Caveat (already in the finding):** no in-tree Python emitter currently produces `type="main"` spans (F02.18); the repro creates one explicitly. The bug is therefore latent in production. The finding's MEDIUM severity already accounts for this.
