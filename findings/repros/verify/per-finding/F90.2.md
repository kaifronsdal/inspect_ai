## What the finding claims

Three duration formatters coexist for sub-minute durations: app-local `formatTime` (`apps/inspect/src/utils/format.ts:6-8` → `5.3 sec`, 1 decimal), `@tsmono/util` `formatTime` (`packages/util/src/format.ts:33-35` → `5 sec`, rounded int), and `formatDurationShort` (`packages/util/src/format.ts:173-181` → `5s`, compact). "Within one sample-detail view, the header pill says `5.3 sec`, the transcript event title for the same span says `5 sec`, and the timeline card says `5s`."

## What the repro's bug_description says to look for

> **Observed:** Three formatters for the same ~2.5-second span: `2.5 sec` vs `3 sec` vs `3s`.
> **Expected:** One duration formatter (or at most two: long + compact) shared across sample header, event panels, and timeline.

## Analysis

### Are they formatting the SAME underlying value differently? — **No.**

Every duration string on screen comes from a **distinct log field**, and each field is rendered by exactly **one** formatter:

| String | Field | Raw | Formatter |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2.8 sec` | `sample.working_time` | 2.818 | app-local (`SampleDisplay.tsx:751`) |
| `3 sec` | `model[1].working_start` | 2.812 | util (`EventTimingPanel.tsx:70`) |
| `3.0 sec` | `stats.completed_at − started_at` | ≈3.0 | app-local (`hooks.tsx:397`) |

The prior re-audit's statement "(A) and (B) format the *same* ~2.81s differently" is **imprecise**: `sample.working_time` (2.818) and `model[1].working_start` (2.812) are different quantities — the former is the sample's total accumulated working time, the latter is the cumulative working time *before* the second model call began. They are 6 ms apart only because the second mockllm call is near-instant and is the last working operation; in a realistic log they would diverge arbitrarily. (C) is a third independent value on the same formatter as (A) and adds nothing.

I found **no code path** where one log field is rendered through two different duration formatters.

### How many formatters does the repro actually exercise? — **Two of three.**

`formatDurationShort` (the `Ns` compact form) is only called from `AgentCardView.tsx:43` and `TimelineMinimap.tsx:186`, both gated on the sample having timeline/sub-agent data. This repro has none (`AgentCardView count=0`), so the third format never appears. The repro's `observed` text "vs `3s`" is not observable.

### Is the underlying finding still valid? — **Yes, as a code-style consistency issue.**

Source confirms three formatters with divergent sub-minute behaviour:
- `apps/inspect/src/utils/format.ts:8` → `` `${formatPrettyDecimal(seconds, 1)} sec` `` (1 decimal)
- `packages/util/src/format.ts:35` → `` `${Math.round(seconds)} sec` `` (integer)
- `packages/util/src/format.ts:175` → `` `${totalSec}s` `` (compact)

Within one sample-detail view a user does see `2.8 sec` (Metadata tab) alongside `3 sec` (Transcript tab) for sub-minute durations — a real but cosmetic inconsistency of *presentation style*, not a case of *one value rendered two ways*.

### What the previous checks got wrong

- **Repro `bug_description`:** claims "three formatters for the same ~2.5-second span" — false on both counts (only two visible; not the same span/value).
- **`checks/F90_2.py`:** treats (A) `2.8 sec` and (B) `3 sec` as "the same elapsed time" — they are different fields.
- **REAUDIT.md:** correctly drops (C) but still asserts (A)≡(B) ("same quantity, Δ=6 ms") — the 6 ms delta *is* the proof they are different fields.

## Recommended action

Keep at **LOW** severity but **reframe the finding** as a pure code-consistency item ("two near-duplicate `formatTime` implementations with different sub-minute precision; consolidate on `@tsmono/util`"). Drop the "same value rendered three ways" impact claim — it is not supported. Fix the repro's `observed` text to stop promising the `3s` format and to stop calling (A)/(B) "the same span".
