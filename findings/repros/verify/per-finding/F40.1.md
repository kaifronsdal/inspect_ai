# F40.1 — RecordTree default-collapse logic never executes

**Prior verdict:** CONFIRMED
**New verdict:** CONFIRMED
**Batch:** 40-content | **Port:** 7649 | **Log:** `2026-04-24T01-25-16-00-00_F40.1-recordtree-never-collapses_Ty2Wwp53XhTr4V9ggxhr4f.eval`

## What the finding claims

`useCollapsibleIds` (`packages/react/src/hooks/useCollapsibleIds.ts:30`) returns `(entries || {})` — never `undefined` — so the guard `if (collapsedIds) return;` at `packages/inspect-components/src/content/RecordTree.tsx:88` always short-circuits, the `defaultCollapsedIds` reducer (depth ≥ `defaultExpandLevel` ∨ `childCount > 5`) never runs, and the flash-prevention `if (!collapsedIds) return null;` at L243 is dead. Net effect: every RecordTree mounts fully expanded regardless of `defaultExpandLevel`.

## What the repro's bug_description says to look for

> **Observed:** The `deep_tree.L1.L2.L3.L4.L5.L6.L7_leaf` branch is fully expanded on first render, and so is `wide_branch` (12 children). No node is collapsed by default.
> **Expected:** Branches deeper than `defaultExpandLevel` and branches with >5 children should mount **collapsed**.

## Analysis

**Source confirms the mechanism exactly as claimed.** `useCollapsibleIds.ts:30` is `return [(entries || {}) as Record<string, boolean>, …]`, and `RecordTree.tsx:87-108` early-returns on truthy `collapsedIds`, so `{}` defeats the effect. The L243 flash guard and its comment ("Don't render until collapsedIds is initialized to avoid flash of all items") are unreachable.

**Browser confirms the symptom.** Without any interaction, depth-7 leaf and all 12 wide-branch children are in the DOM; zero nodes are collapsed.

**Is "always expanded" actually a problem, or preferable?** It is a problem, not a preference:
- The code *explicitly intends* to collapse — there's a `defaultExpandLevel` prop, a depth/childCount heuristic, and a flash-guard comment. This is dead-by-bug, not by design.
- Six call sites pass `defaultExpandLevel={0}` expecting "start fully collapsed" (`ScoreEventView.tsx:73`, `ScoreEditEventView.tsx:124`, `ChatMessage.tsx:140`, `ContentDataView.tsx:40`, `ServerToolCall.tsx:117`, `SampleScoresGrid.tsx:120`). All of them are silently ignored.
- For this 25-row toy tree the result is merely a bit busy; for real `store` payloads (the explicit motivation in the finding) it produces a wall of rows on every mount.

The prior CONFIRMED verdict stands; nothing was overstated.

## Recommended action

Keep as-is (HIGH). Fix per the finding's suggestion: have `useCollapsibleIds` return `entries` un-coalesced (typed `Record<string, boolean> | undefined`), or replace the guard with an `initialized` ref so the default-collapse effect runs exactly once.
